Salwana

Inventory management, merchant finance tracking, product reservation, and online marketplace for small and medium-sized merchants.

Project introduction

Salwana is an inventory management, merchant finance tracking, product reservation, and online marketplace application designed for small and medium-sized merchants. It is a personal application I developed end to end.

Problem it aims to solve

Small and medium-sized merchants often manage inventory, finances, orders, and reservations with fragmented or manual tools. Salwana brings these workflows together into one application, covering catalog and inventory, suppliers and restocking, orders, reservations, payments, and merchant finance.

Application purpose

The purpose of Salwana is to give merchants a single, role-based platform to run their day-to-day operations — from stocking and selling products to tracking money, handling reservations, and serving consumers through an online marketplace — while supporting suppliers and administrators in the same system.

Main features

The application is feature-complete across 16 build-order modules, implemented end to end against real Cloud Functions and Firestore:

  • Authentication and role-based access control
  • Catalog and inventory management
  • Supplier and restocking management
  • Orders and order status tracking
  • Product reservations
  • Payments (GCash and Maya simulated flows, plus Cash on Delivery)
  • Reviews and ratings
  • Consumer discovery and marketplace browsing
  • Merchant finance tracking
  • Notifications (push via Firebase Cloud Messaging)
  • Dashboards and analytics
  • Subscriptions
  • Reports and export (PDF, Excel)
  • Administration panel
  • Barcode / QR scanning

Supported user roles

Consumer

Browses the marketplace, places orders, makes reservations, and leaves reviews.

Merchant

Manages inventory and catalog, finances, orders, reservations, and subscriptions.

Supplier

Handles supply and restocking workflows with merchants.

Administrator

Oversees the platform through the admin panel, reports, and analytics.

Each role has its own dashboard, navigation, and permissions.

Technologies and architecture

Client: Flutter (Dart SDK ^3.3.0), with Riverpod for state management and go_router for routing.

Cloud backend (Firebase): Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Firebase Storage, Firebase Cloud Functions, and Firebase Cloud Messaging.

Supporting libraries: google_fonts, shimmer, cached_network_image, fl_chart, flutter_svg, intl, uuid, connectivity_plus, shared_preferences, image_picker, qr_flutter, mobile_scanner, pdf, printing, excel, path_provider, share_plus, flutter_local_notifications, url_launcher, http.

Architecture:

  • lib/core — theme, shared widgets, router, Riverpod providers, utils
  • lib/models — one file per Firestore entity (mirrors firestore.rules)
  • lib/features/{auth,consumer,merchant,supplier,admin} — one folder per role/module, each with data/ (repositories) and presentation/ (screens)
  • functions/src — Cloud Functions grouped by module
  • firestore.rules, storage.rules, firestore.indexes.json — backend security and indexing

Responsive and adaptive UI: grids compute column counts from available width (2 on phones, 3 on tablets, 4 on desktop), navigation switches between a bottom bar and a side NavigationRail, and forms are capped at a readable width. Firestore persistence keeps the app usable offline, with an app-wide offline banner.
