TranSync

Smarter transportation, simplified — an integrated public transportation system built for Pampanga commuters.

Visit TranSync Website ↗

Project introduction

TranSync is a smart transportation platform developed to improve the commuting experience through technology. It gives users a more convenient way to access transportation information, routes, and mobility services — helping create a more organized transportation experience. The official project website is available at transync.infinityfreeapp.com.

Application overview

TranSync connects commuters with smarter transportation solutions, making everyday travel more convenient, organized, and accessible. It is built around the needs of everyday commuters and covers jeepney, UV Express, and bus routes across Pampanga.

Problem it aims to solve

Commuters often struggle to find transportation options, know available routes, deal with waiting uncertainty, and access reliable transportation information. TranSync addresses these challenges by bringing transportation information together in one platform.

Purpose and objectives

Technology-driven mobility: TranSync uses technology to make transportation information more accessible, organized, and easy to understand for everyday commuters. Its goal is to create a smarter, more convenient, and user-friendly transportation experience for commuters across Pampanga.

Key features

  • Smart Route Information — helps users understand available jeepney, UV Express, and bus routes clearly and quickly.
  • Real-Time Tracking — provides live vehicle and location information where available.
  • Digital Fare Management — makes fare-related processes more convenient and organized.
  • UV & Bus Booking — reserve a seat on UV Express and bus trips ahead of time, right from the app.
  • User-Friendly Experience — designed with simplicity and accessibility in mind.
  • Connected Transportation — brings transportation information together in one platform.

Intended users

Everyday commuters in Pampanga, including users of jeepney, UV Express, and bus services. The platform is also relevant to transport stakeholders such as drivers, operators, and associations, as reflected in the original project concept.
